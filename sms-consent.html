Effective Date: October 29, 2026  ·  aproposgroupllc.com

What You Are Consenting To

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you are giving Apropos Group LLC and its operating division Stellar Intelligent Technologies permission to send you text messages (SMS) related to our services.

Sender: Apropos Group LLC / Stellar Intelligent Technologies · Las Vegas, NV

Purpose: Service notifications, case updates, appointment confirmations, and business communications related to an active service engagement.

Frequency: Message frequency varies. You will receive messages triggered by service activity — not unsolicited bulk promotions.

Cost: Message and data rates may apply (carrier charges).

Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You'll receive one confirmation, then no further messages.

Help: Reply HELP or email jmitchell1126@gmail.com

How We Obtain Consent

We obtain your consent through one of the following methods before sending any SMS messages:

  • Web form opt-in — You check a clearly labeled SMS consent checkbox on a form and provide your mobile number
  • Verbal consent — You verbally agree to receive SMS updates during a phone interaction with our service system (Ava, our AI property management assistant)
  • Reply YES — You reply YES to an initial opt-in invitation message sent to your number at your prior request

Consent is not required as a condition of purchasing any product or service.

What We Will Not Do

  • We will not share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing purposes
  • We will not send messages unrelated to an active service engagement or your opt-in request
  • We will not charge you for messages (carrier rates may apply; those are carrier charges)
  • We will not ignore an opt-out request

How to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any message
  • Email jmitchell1126@gmail.com with your number and a request to be removed
  • Call (888) 244-5737

Withdrawal takes effect immediately. One confirmation SMS will be sent; no further messages will follow.
