Program Description

Apropos Group LLC and its operating division Stellar Intelligent Technologies (collectively "we," "our," or "us") operate an SMS messaging program to deliver service notifications, case status updates, and business communications to customers and service contacts who have opted in.

Messages are sent from telephone numbers operated by Apropos Group LLC / Stellar Intelligent Technologies, including numbers provisioned through Twilio Inc. on our behalf.

Message Types

You may receive the following types of messages when you opt in:

  • Service request confirmations and case status updates
  • Appointment scheduling notifications and reminders
  • Business service updates and operational alerts
  • Responses to inquiries submitted by phone or web form
  • Follow-up communications related to an active service engagement

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your service activity. Transactional messages (case updates, confirmations) are sent as triggered by service events. You will not receive unsolicited promotional bulk messages.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. These charges are assessed by your mobile carrier and are separate from any service fees charged by Apropos Group LLC.

How to Opt In

You may opt in to SMS communications by:

  • Providing your mobile number and checking the SMS consent box on a web form
  • Verbally consenting during a phone call with our service system
  • Responding YES to an initial opt-in confirmation message sent to your number

Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service or product.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message from us
  • Contacting us directly at jmitchell1126@gmail.com

After replying STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message. No further messages will be sent to that number unless you re-enroll.
