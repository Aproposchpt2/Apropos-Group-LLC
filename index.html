What We Do

Four disciplines. One standard of engineering.

We operate where reliability is non-negotiable — and we hold every engagement to the same standard, whether it's a campus-wide voice migration or a production AI system.

01

Unified Communications Engineering

Enterprise voice, contact center, and collaboration — Cisco UC (CUCM, Unity, UCCX, CUBE) and Microsoft Teams Enterprise Voice, with SIP trunking, SBC, E911, and Direct Routing — architected for scale, continuity, and compliance.

02

AI Automation & Intelligent Systems

Production artificial intelligence built on modern large-language-model and cloud architecture — automating workflows, surfacing intelligence, and turning operational data into decisions agencies and enterprises can act on.

03

Government Technology & Modernization

Secure, compliant systems that move organizations from legacy to modern. We design, build, and sustain to public-sector standards — bridging aging infrastructure to current platforms without the risk of disruption.

04

Enterprise Infrastructure & Integration

Networks, gateways, and integrations that connect legacy and cloud. We consolidate cost and complexity while increasing reliability — the unglamorous backbone that everything else depends on.

Proven In The Field

Delivered where downtime isn't an option.

  • Migrated 2,000+ enterprise users across 100+ departments to modern unified communications — with zero unplanned service interruptions.
  • Engineered enterprise contact-center and IVR platforms for a multi-campus higher-education system — 100+ call queues, in continuous operation.
  • Deployed voice infrastructure for a U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs medical facility — clinical and administrative telephony from design through cutover.
  • Architected production AI platforms for government procurement intelligence — modern LLM, secure authentication, and cloud-native automation.
  • Led accelerated collaboration deployments under pressure — keeping mission-critical operations running through rapid, high-stakes transitions.
