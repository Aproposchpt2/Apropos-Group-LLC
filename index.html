Conversational Procurement Experience

A Guided Experience Built Around the Business

APROPOS platforms are designed to reduce contractor effort. Rather than requiring users to begin with complex procurement searches, the guided experience begins with the business.

“We do not begin with contracts. We begin with your business.”

The Procurement Advisor welcomes the contractor, learns about the business, explains the process, supports profile creation, and guides the user toward a personalized opportunity dashboard.

The experience organizes the procurement journey into a clear sequence while keeping official solicitation documents and contractor verification at the center of every decision.

01

Prepare the Business

Capture capabilities, qualifications, services, registrations, and the operational profile needed to evaluate opportunity.

02

Match the Opportunity

Route the contractor toward relevant procurement opportunities aligned with the business profile.

03

Understand the Requirements

Help the contractor examine fit, verification needs, potential gaps, and the questions that matter before bidding.

Decision Intelligence & Guided Support

Understand the Requirements Before Making the Commitment

APROPOS extends opportunity discovery with additional decision intelligence and guided support services designed to help contractors investigate opportunities responsibly.

Analyze Fit

Additional report · $15 one-time

Analyze Fit provides additional procurement decision intelligence for a selected opportunity, including apparent alignment, potential gaps, requirements requiring verification, important questions to investigate, and bid/no-bid considerations.

Required disclosure: Analyze Fit does not replace the official solicitation, establish eligibility, or guarantee a contract award.
